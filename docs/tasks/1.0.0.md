# Siftail `1.0.0` evidence gates

**Status:** Planned
**Release state:** Unreleased
**Task IDs:** SFT-059–SFT-063
**Milestone authority:** `PRODUCT.md` §19.6

`1.0.0` is earned by operational evidence, not new feature count or a calendar
date. These tasks gather and close evidence after the public dogfood gate;
post-`1.0` product candidates are intentionally excluded.

---

### SFT-059 — Establish the multi-month production dogfood record

**Status:** Planned
**Milestone:** 1.0.0
**Depends on:** SFT-058
**Issue/PR:** —

**Authoritative references:** `PRODUCT.md` §§19.6, 20; `ARCHITECTURE.md`
§§35–36, 41.

**Outcome:** Maintain a privacy-safe, reviewable record that Siftail has served
real production logging needs for several months.

**Acceptance:**

- The record spans multiple months and identifies released versions,
  architectures, workload ranges, upgrades, maintenance, outages, and recovery
  events without storing application payloads or credentials.
- It tracks ingestion outcomes, availability, resource envelopes, retention,
  backup verification, restore drills, and operator friction using bounded
  summaries.
- Incidents and limitations link to focused fixes or explicit accepted risk;
  missing observation is not replaced by synthetic soak evidence.

**Verification:**

- Periodically review production health, local measurements, backup
  verification, and issue history using the documented dogfood procedure.
- At closure, reconcile the observation window with release notes, migrations,
  incidents, and known issues and record dates and evidence sources.

**Impact:** Evidence and documentation only. No telemetry is added; production
observations remain local, sanitized, and deliberately recorded.

### SFT-060 — Close data-loss risks, incidents, and recovery drills

**Status:** Planned
**Milestone:** 1.0.0
**Depends on:** SFT-059
**Issue/PR:** —

**Authoritative references:** `PRODUCT.md` §19.6; `DOMAIN.md` §§26, 28–31;
`ARCHITECTURE.md` §§8, 10, 24–25.

**Outcome:** Demonstrate that no known data-loss defect remains and that
documented recovery works against failures observed or plausibly encountered
during dogfooding.

**Acceptance:**

- Every durability, corruption, loss, acknowledgement, retention, backup, and
  restore incident is resolved, explicitly disproven, or blocks `1.0`.
- Recovery drills cover current full/configuration backups, rollback copies,
  disk-full mode, interrupted maintenance, failed upgrade, and host restart.
- Restored data passes integrity and critical application queries, and operator
  instructions match the performed drills.
- No statement implies forensic deletion or storage guarantees beyond SQLite
  and the host filesystem.

**Verification:**

- Re-run destructive fault/recovery suites and perform documented restore and
  rollback drills on copies of representative production-shaped data.
- Review acknowledgement/durability tests, issue history, security audit
  outcomes, and operator docs; record exact results and remaining risks.

**Impact:** Evidence plus focused defect/documentation fixes as needed. Any
unresolved known data-loss defect prevents completion.

### SFT-061 — Prove migration and backup compatibility

**Status:** Planned
**Milestone:** 1.0.0
**Depends on:** SFT-058
**Issue/PR:** —

**Authoritative references:** `PRODUCT.md` §19.6; `ARCHITECTURE.md` §§10, 25,
31, 35; repository migration rules.

**Outcome:** Demonstrate repeated forward upgrades and backup recovery across
the supported public dogfood history.

**Acceptance:**

- Every supported released schema fixture migrates to the candidate with
  representative data preserved, integrity passing, and critical reads/writes
  working.
- Full and configuration-only backups from every documented compatible release
  verify and restore according to the versioned contract.
- Older releases refuse newer schemas, active sessions are never restored, and
  rollback procedures remain valid.
- Compatibility windows and any intentionally unsupported artifacts are
  explicit before the final release decision.

**Verification:**

- Run the complete release-to-candidate migration/backup matrix on both
  supported architectures and production packaging.
- Repeat selected paths from clean artifacts and record versions, digests,
  fixture provenance, commands, results, and limitations.

**Impact:** Compatibility evidence and fixtures. Schema or format changes
discovered here require their own reviewed migration/contract work.

### SFT-062 — Demonstrate sustained resource and contract stability

**Status:** Planned
**Milestone:** 1.0.0
**Depends on:** SFT-059
**Issue/PR:** —

**Authoritative references:** `PRODUCT.md` §§17, 19.6; `ARCHITECTURE.md`
§§31–33, 36.

**Outcome:** Show that resource targets and essential ingestion/configuration
contracts remained stable through production dogfooding.

**Acceptance:**

- Multi-month evidence and repeatable benchmarks show bounded RSS, goroutines,
  queue/client state, disk/WAL behavior, retention, and maintenance costs.
- Sustained and burst ingestion plus normal p95 commit targets are remeasured on
  supported production images; regressions are explained or block the gate.
- Ingestion endpoints/formats, token binding, acknowledgement semantics,
  canonical events, historical URLs/cursors, backup formats, and essential
  configuration compatibility are reviewed for stability.
- Any required breaking change is resolved before `1.0`, documented with
  migration/compatibility impact, and re-observed rather than waived.

**Verification:**

- Re-run production benchmarks, 10-million-event queries, soak, concurrent
  session/read/Live/maintenance stress, and resource-ledger reconciliation.
- Compare methods and results to the public dogfood baseline and record
  hardware-specific limitations without overgeneralization.

**Impact:** Evidence and compatibility documentation. Optimization or contract
changes require separate focused implementation and review.

### SFT-063 — Close the final operational and `1.0.0` release gate

**Status:** Planned
**Milestone:** 1.0.0
**Depends on:** SFT-060, SFT-061, SFT-062
**Issue/PR:** —

**Authoritative references:** `PRODUCT.md` §§19.6, 20–21;
`ARCHITECTURE.md` §§35–36, 41; `AGENTS.md` §§29–30.

**Outcome:** Make the evidence-based maintainer decision that Siftail's
operational behavior, documentation, contracts, and product boundary merit
`1.0.0`.

**Acceptance:**

- The final matrix covers every `PRODUCT.md` §19.6 condition and repository
  stable-release gate with current automated, measured, production, and manual
  evidence.
- Common install, upgrade, administration, ingestion, History/Live, retention,
  diagnostics, backup/restore, export, failure, and recovery operations are
  documented and repeatedly pass.
- No known data-loss or critical security defect remains; resource and
  essential compatibility contracts are stable and known issues are candid.
- The maintainer explicitly reviews and reaffirms the product exclusions and
  can reject incompatible expansion without unresolved foundational gaps.
- Task completion records a release recommendation; tagging and publishing
  remain separate explicit maintainer actions.

**Verification:**

- Run the complete stable-release checklist on final production artifacts:
  all tests/race/browser/migrations/recovery, Coolify/generic ingestion,
  saturation/disk-full/shutdown, benchmarks, soak, security/accessibility, and
  documentation procedures.
- Record exact versions, digests, commands, environments, results, evidence
  links, manual decisions, and limitations; independently check the requirement
  matrix for omissions.

**Impact:** Final gate evidence and release documentation. It adds no features,
migration, dependency, tag, or publication by itself.
