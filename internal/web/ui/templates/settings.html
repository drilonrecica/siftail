Scope and deletion order

These limits apply globally to retained application events. They do not delete Servers, ingestion-token metadata, aliases, administrator sessions, settings, or security audit history.

Cleanup removes the oldest eligible events first, ordered by the earlier of event time and receive time, then internal event ID. Work is performed in bounded transactions.

Size cleanup starts near 95% of the configured target and works toward 90%. One admitted batch and transient WAL activity can temporarily exceed the target.

Deletion limitation

Retention reduces Siftail's application-log history. It is not forensic erasure and does not control backups, snapshots, or unrelated host files.
